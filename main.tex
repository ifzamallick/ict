\section{Introduction}
This report provides a concise overview of fundamental mechanical energy principles, focusing specifically on kinetic energy and its core applications in classical physics. Understanding how energy is stored and transferred is essential for analyzing motion and work within mechanical systems.
\section{Main Points}
\begin{enumerate}
    \textbf{Definition of Energy:} Energy is the quantitative property that must be transferred to an object in order to perform work on, 
    or to heat, the object.
    \item  Kinetic energy represents the energy possessed by an object due to its motion, depending directly on both mass and velocity.
    \item  \textbf{Conservation Principles:} Total mechanical energy in an isolated system remains constant if only conservative forces are acting.
    
\end{enumerate}
